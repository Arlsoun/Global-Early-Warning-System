Project Title:
Explainable Dengue Early Warning and Decision Support System

Problem:
Dengue outbreaks can place significant pressure on communities and healthcare systems. Early identification of increasing outbreak risk can help decision-makers prioritize surveillance, prevention, and resources.

Objective:
Develop an explainable data-driven system that analyzes historical dengue, weather, geographic, and demographic information to estimate dengue outbreak risk and provide understandable evidence for each risk assessment.

Core Questions:

Which locations show increasing dengue risk?
Which environmental and epidemiological factors are associated with increased risk?
How accurately can historical data predict periods of elevated risk?
How early can the system identify an increase in risk?
How can the system explain its risk assessments to decision-makers?

Important Principle:
The system will support human decision-making. It will not replace public-health professionals or make medical decisions.
